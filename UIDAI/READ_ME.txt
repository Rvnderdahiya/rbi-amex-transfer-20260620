UIDAI INVENTORY AND ORIGINAL DOCUMENTS

Cutoff: 5 November 2026. Review package prepared 7 November 2026.

OPEN UIDAI_Inventory.xlsx. The single Inventory sheet has the seven requested fields.
Click the document title to open its supplied PDF. The UIDAI cell opens the official
listing. Keep the workbook and Originals folder together after extracting the ZIP.
Additional language/version files are in the title cell note and Audit/Inventory.csv.
Names are short for reliable Windows extraction. Full publisher titles are preserved
in the workbook and traceability files. No admin rights or assembly script is needed.

SCOPE AND COUNTS
All listed items in Circulars, Notifications, Acts, Rules, Updated Regulations and
Gazetted Notifications were retained. No UIDAI subject/HR/status exclusions were
specified. 101 source items become 100 inventory rows after consolidating one exact
duplicate SOP listing. One additional Gazette pagination appearance repeats an item.
113 captured attachment URLs yield 107 distinct PDF payloads. Identical bilingual
payloads are stored once; all URL/language relationships are preserved. An original
2020 Gazette was recovered from an older official UIDAI attachment, giving 108
distinct instrument PDFs. One additional RTI disclosure PDF documents that recovery.
Different compilations, amendments and historical originals are NOT title-deduped.

READING THE FIELDS
Name: publisher listing title, with a portable link to the document.
Date: printed instrument/cover date. For compiled Acts/regulations it is the base
instrument date; compilation/version dates are in the Date note and Audit/Inventory.csv.
Issue date is NOT automatically the upload, Gazette publication, signature or effective
date. Three documents have no complete issue date; one gives only September 2026.
Issuing Authority: actual issuing body in the instrument, not automatically the host.
Nature: instrument type, rather than assuming every PDF is a circular.
Website name: UIDAI, linked to the official source listing.
Applicability for AEBC: preliminary document-specific scope assessment. Conditional
means an actual AEBC role/workflow must be confirmed, not a confirmed Yes. Internal
UIDAI notices are labelled as having no direct AEBC banking duty identified.
Addressee: printed recipients where established, otherwise explicitly labelled scope
audience. It must not be read as a fabricated formal To line.

IMPORTANT REVIEW POINTS
U015: issue day blank, signed 18 September 2026. Name-update supersession is limited
to the name process; do not automatically discard the entire 2021 gender procedure.
U018: listing title says 21 September 2025; the PDF says 19 September 2025. PDF date used.
U022/U027/U028: issue day not established; programme year/upload date not substituted.
U036: two-page circular refers to an Attorney General opinion annexure, but the
publisher-supplied PDF does not include it. The separate opinion was not recovered
from the official sources searched. Do not call that referenced annexure delivered.
U049: English/Hindi employee-service compilations have different as-of dates.
U051: English/Hindi authentication compilations have different as-of labels.
U093: current Gazette link supplies an updated Hindi compilation. Both it and the
recovered 2020 original are retained. UIDAI RTI disclosure provides recovery provenance.
U017: the one-year fee-waiver window from 1 October 2025 is not proof of a continuing
waiver at the cutoff. Historical policy/fee versions must be read with later changes.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Original bytes and hashes are preserved. Every unique listed PDF was rendered in
MuPDF and independently in PDFium. Selected metadata/scope pages were visually
reviewed. Some original PDFs have font/structure/Flate warnings, retained in audit
evidence. Successful opening does not prove word-for-word bilingual equivalence or
the publisher's substantive accuracy. No screenshots are substitutes for originals.
Applicability/current legal force require compliance confirmation of AEBC's actual
Registrar, AUA/KUA, offline-verifier, APB/AePS or other relevant role. This package is
not an all-India legal completeness certificate and does not alter the RBI/KMT work.

Audit contains source/attachment mapping, hashes, review reasons and exceptions.
