SCOPE AND RECONCILIATION
Both What's New and Archives were collected, with the homepage What's New checked
for additional notices. 231 list-item appearances reconcile to 213 announcements.
18 repeated listing appearances were consolidated using matching titles and identical
complete attachment sets. Amendments and differing original files are not merged.
216 original URLs represent 215 distinct payloads: one excluded legal-consultant
notice has two different URLs returning identical PDF bytes. All aliases remain in
Attachment_URL_reconciliation.json. This is not an unaccounted missing file.
One announcement may have multiple attachments. 31 announcements meet a supplied
title/body exclusion, leaving 182 inventory rows. The package supplies 183 original
PDFs (942 pages) and one original XLS. These are 184 files, not 184 announcements.
One retained row, F212, is a portal-only announcement and offers no attachment.
Its saved listing extract is not represented as an original PDF. F031 was recovered.

Phrases were checked in titles AND available full document text, using case,
whitespace and punctuation normalisation with word boundaries. They were not
expanded to all hiring, tenders or all similarly worded phrases. For example,
'Applications are invited for the post of' differs from the supplied phrase.
Consequently some recruitment and procurement notices remain, with no direct AEBC
banking duty identified. Exclusion evidence, source URLs and hashes are in Audit.
Excluded originals remain preserved in the source archive, outside this review pack.

FIELDS
Name: publisher title, linked to the unchanged original or clearly labelled exception.
Date: original instrument/cover issue date where established. 147 full dates, 5 partial
dates and 30 dates not established. Event, deadline, contract period, referenced OM,
upload, signature and filename dates are not automatically substituted. Notes explain
the basis, conflicts and missing dates. Partial dates remain text, not invented days.
Issuing Authority: actual issuing body, distinguished from the hosting website.
Nature: instrument/notice type; procurement, consultation and press releases are not
mislabelled as banking regulations.
Website name: official FIU source hyperlink.
Applicability for AEBC: preliminary document-scope reasoning. Potentially relevant
requires compliance confirmation; Conditional requires confirmation of actual AEBC
activity. No direct banking duty identified does not mean the source was deleted.
Addressee: printed recipients where established; otherwise explicitly labelled scope
audience. A scope audience is not a verbatim formal To block.

REVIEW POINTS
F031: published URL contains an extra space before .pdf and returns Request Rejected
HTML. The same official FIU filename without that space returns the genuine original.
The whole recovered page was visually read: subject, issuer, reference and 11 April 2023
issue date confirmed. Failed-link response and successful-recovery hashes remain in
Audit/Publisher. No error page or reconstructed PDF is substituted for an original.
F212: a link to the FINnet portal only; the list offers no attached instrument.
F045 is a March 2022 draft consultation. F213 is the later September 2022 final hearing
policy. Both are retained and distinguished.
F060-F062 are historic 2020 PML amendment notifications. Their issue, publication and
period-specific relief dates must not be conflated with current obligations.
F106: cover states December 2017 although surrounding tender/corrigenda refer to
March-May 2017. The publisher's inconsistency is disclosed, not silently repaired.
F139: historical FCRA association register, not proof of current registration.
VDA/TCSP instructions are conditional on actual covered service-provider activity,
not automatically applicable to every bank. MoUs are cooperation information.
References to separately circulated tender materials are not a claim that unpublished
or bidder-email-only materials were downloaded.

CHECKS AND LIMITS
Every supplied original is byte-for-byte SHA256 matched to its captured source.
All 942 retained PDF pages opened and rendered in MuPDF and PDFium. 25 supplied PDFs
produce publisher-structure warnings; 10 require MuPDF reader-side recovery. They also
render in independent PDFium. Original bytes are preserved; no repaired derivative
is presented as the original. Per-file warnings are in independent_pdf_checks.json.
Native text, Hindi/English OCR and Office extraction support full-document phrase screening.
38 documents/113 weak or missing text pages received additional OCR. First-page
contact sheets and targeted low-text/date/substantive pages were visually inspected.
Photos, blank publisher pages and blank form fields were reviewed and preserved.
This is not a human word-for-word certification of all 942 pages, a guarantee against
OCR omissions, a final AEBC applicability decision or a current-force legal opinion.
Machine quality flags remain in the audit evidence rather than being relabelled clear.
Native Excel and portable-extraction checks are recorded separately in Audit.
No screenshots are supplied as substitutes for original documents.
