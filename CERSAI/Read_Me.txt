CERSAI REVIEW PACKAGE
Collection cutoff: 5 November 2026

WHAT IS INCLUDED
162 captured public source entries (103 English, 59 Hindi). The inventory
retains 96 announcements and records 66 exclusions separately.
These are not 96 unique laws. Historical messages, technical files,
recruitment notices not caught by the four rules, and language variants remain.

OPEN THE PACKAGE
Extract the complete ZIP into a short folder named CERSAI in Documents.
Open CERSAI_Inventory.xlsx. Keep Originals, Announcements, Supporting and Audit
beside the workbook. Click a name for the supplied file or saved announcement.
Click CERSAI in Website name for the official listing. Cell notes preserve
date evidence and supporting-file names. No absolute computer paths are used.

SEVEN COLUMNS
Name: publisher announcement text, preserved without replacing it with a guessed title.
Date: printed notice date when stated. For webpage-only messages/technical
listing entries, publisher announcement date is identified in the cell note.
Twelve inventory entries without a stated original issuance date remain Not stated; effective,
meeting, deadline, coverage-year and filename dates are not silently substituted.
Issuing Authority: actual issuer/technical author where supported, not merely the host.
Nature: distinguishes notifications, operational messages, formats and recruitment.
Website name: official source link.
Applicability for AEBC: preliminary reason and business condition, not legal sign-off.
Addressee: explicit recipient or clearly labelled inferred audience.

FILTERS AND TRACEABILITY
Maintenance, RFP, Bid, Website will not be available. Case-insensitive whole
words/phrases are checked in publisher title/short title and document content.
Fulltext and graphic/low-text checks support the document decisions. OCR alone
is not a conclusive negative. No extra Hindi equivalents or general recruitment
exclusions were added. Expiry/hidden flags do not establish legal withdrawal.
No prior RBI/KMT active-only or scope filters are imported into this tranche.

   PDF downloads or invented PDFs. The audit retains the full source messages.
7. A 2021 excluded RFP link returns a 2024 corrigendum. Captured as-served bytes
   are retained for traceability, not certified as the requested historical notice.

CHECKS
All 65 captured document/archive payload hashes match the preserved originals.
All 55 PDF files / 1,484 pages rendered in both MuPDF and PDFium. Office XML/ZIP
integrity and archive-member identity were checked. This is not digital-signature
validation or certification of legal status. Native Excel and portable-package
checks are recorded in Audit once completed.

contains exclusion evidence. Audit/document_review.json contains assessments,

not silently overwritten. No guarantee of historical material no longer offered
