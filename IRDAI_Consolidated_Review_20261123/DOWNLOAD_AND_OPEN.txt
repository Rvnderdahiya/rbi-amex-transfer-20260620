IRDAI Consolidated Review Inventory

Download every ZIP, Transfer_Manifest.json, Extract_Inventory.ps1 and EXTRACT_INVENTORY.bat into one short folder.
Double-click EXTRACT_INVENTORY.bat. It verifies all ZIP hashes, extracts locally and verifies every delivered file. No network access, Python, Git or administrator rights are needed.
If PowerShell is blocked by company policy, do not bypass the policy. Use Windows Extract All on each ZIP to the SAME short destination. Each ZIP is an ordinary independent archive.
Open IRDAI_Reviewed/IRDAI_Reviewed_Inventory.xlsx. Keep Originals and Audit beside the workbook. The document title opens the local PDF; Website opens the official source.
This supersedes manual joining of the named previous batches, not the unreviewed source universe. Do not add it to the old batch totals. Known exceptions remain flagged.
